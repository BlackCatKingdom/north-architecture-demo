Studio / Approach

STUDIO.

NØRTH explores quiet architecture through residential, cultural and small public projects. The studio language is deliberately restrained: direct typography, quiet color, strong imagery and a small information architecture.

DESIGN
THROUGH
REDUCTION.

Our concept practice works from program, site and material before style. We prefer a small number of decisions that can carry the whole project.

Architecture is treated as a system of relationships: public and private, open and enclosed, heavy and light, permanent and adaptable.
