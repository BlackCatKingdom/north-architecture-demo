Retreat / Bergen

RIDGE PAVILION.

A single folded roof that belongs to the slope.

LocationBergen
TypeRetreat
Year2025
Area86 m²

ONE CLEAR
SPATIAL IDEA.

The pavilion is reduced to one roof plane, one sheltered edge and one framed view. Entry happens from the high side of the site and the building gradually opens toward the valley. The structure is deliberately legible so that roof, room and route read as the same architectural decision.
